INITIAL DOCUMENTED CANDIDATES

1. Controller - Seeed Studio XIAO ESP32-S3 Sense, SKU 113991115
Candidate selected for research. Official nominal envelope: 21 x 17.8 x 15 mm with expansion board. Exact baseboard/expansion/camera revision remains to be frozen. Reserve camera, microphone and SD resources; preserve USB, antenna, battery-pad and SD access. Do not use a bare ESP32-S3 module footprint.
Evidence:
https://www.seeedstudio.com/XIAO-ESP32S3-Sense-p-5639.html
https://wiki.seeedstudio.com/xiao_esp32s3_getting_started/
Next check: download the matching revision drawings; verify both 7-pin rows, underside battery connections and stack height against the selected revision.

2. IMU - Adafruit BNO055, original non-STEMMA QT board, product 2472
Provisional candidate; do not substitute the newer QT layout. Product listing gives 20 x 27 x 4 mm, mounting-hole spacing 20 x 12 mm and addresses 0x28/0x29. Vendor explicitly warns that original and QT pin orders differ.
Electrical evidence: VIN accepts 3.3-5 V, 3VO is a regulator OUTPUT, SDA/SCL each have 10 kohm pull-ups. Do not connect 3VO to another regulator output.
CRITICAL HOLD: Vendor reports BNO055 I2C interoperability problems with ESP32-S3. Retain BNO055 but investigate UART operation or a validated interface solution in Step 4. A generic I2C mux is not an established fix. Final carrier connections cannot yet be locked.
Evidence:
https://www.adafruit.com/product/2472
https://learn.adafruit.com/adafruit-bno055-absolute-orientation-sensor/pinouts

3. Environment - Adafruit BME280, product 2652, STEMMA QT revision
Candidate for documented procurement, not a proven replacement for the original six-pin breakout. Current board envelope 25.2 x 18.0 x 4.6 mm. Vendor provides regulator/level shifting; proposed carrier supply is 3.3 V. Default I2C address 0x77; verify address straps. Physical board changed in the QT revision even though vendor says header pinout is unchanged.
HOLD: map VIN/GND/SCK/SDI/SDO/CS by manufacturer CAD, never by the original generic connector ordering. Compare size and location with original assembly before adopting.
Evidence:
https://www.adafruit.com/product/2652
https://learn.adafruit.com/adafruit-bme280-humidity-barometric-pressure-temperature-sensor-breakout/pinouts

4. Audio - DFRobot DFPlayer Mini, DFR0299
Candidate selected for research. Vendor specifies 3.2-5 V input, nominal 20 x 20 mm. Provide local bulk and ceramic decoupling. Carrier power architecture must maintain the specified voltage through battery discharge. Preserve access to the module's separate microSD slot.
HOLD: inspect manufacturer dimension/pin-map drawing before establishing two header rows or pin numbering. Confirm speaker impedance/current and maximum intended output. Source page UART framing text contains an apparent inconsistency; use datasheet/protocol documentation rather than copying it uncritically.
Evidence:
https://wiki.dfrobot.com/dfr0299

5. Display - EastRising ER-OLEDM013-1W-I2C
Research candidate: 1.3 inch 128 x 64 white SH1106 I2C module, advertised for 3.3 V. Manufacturer search result confirms model/function; direct page fetch returned 403. Dimensions, four-pin order, mounting holes and electrical ratings NOT verified. Do not adopt footprint yet.
The Waveshare 10451 alternative was screened out as a direct replacement: it has an eight-pin interface and a different board outline, requiring a deliberate redesign.
Evidence:
https://www.buydisplay.com/i2c-white-1-3-inch-oled-display-module-128x64-arduino-raspberry-pi
https://www.waveshare.com/product/ai/displays/oled/1.3inch-oled-b.htm
